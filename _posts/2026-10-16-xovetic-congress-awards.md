The annual XoveTIC congress was held at CITIC on October 15 and 16, bringing together early-career researchers in Information and Communication Technologies to showcase their scientific work through oral presentations and posters

Three research contributions related to the project were featured at the congress. Lucas Grandal Lama and Fernando Mondragón Sampedro delivered their oral presentations “Evolutionary Optimization for Quantum Machine Learning in Sleep Medicine” and “Performance Evaluation of Quantum Support Vector Machines under Realistic Noise and Mitigation” respectively, while Samuel Otero Agraso presented his poster “Patient-Level Evaluation of EEG-Based Depression Detection”.

The team's participation was marked by several awards across different categories: Lucas received the award for Best Work in Quantum Technologies, Fernando was recognized for Best Oral Presentation, and Samuel received the award for Most Significant Contribution in Basic and Theoretical Research.
