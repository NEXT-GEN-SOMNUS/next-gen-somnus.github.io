# Project Researchers Receive Three Awards at the XoveTIC Conference

The annual XoveTIC conference was held at CITIC on October 15th and 16th, bringing together early-career researchers in Information and Communication Technologies (ICT) to present their scientific work through oral presentations and posters.

Lucas Grandal Lama represented the project with his contribution, "Evolutionary Optimization for Quantum Machine Learning in Sleep Medicine". His work explores the use of evolutionary optimizers in quantum machine learning algorithms to investigate whether they offer advantages over classical optimization methods. Other researchers associated with the project also took part in the conference. Fernando Mondragón Sampedro presented the poster "Performance Evaluation of Quantum Support Vector Machines under Realistic Noise and Mitigation" and Samuel Otero Agraso presented "Patient-Level Evaluation of EEG-Based Depression Detection".

The team's participation was marked by three awards across different categories. Lucas received the award for Best Work in Quantum Technologies, while Fernando was recognized for Best Oral Presentation. The third award went to Samuel, in the category of Most Significant Contribution in Basic and Theoretical Research.
