CITIC at the University of A Coruña took part last thursday, september 17, in the second edition of GALIA TECH, held at the Lux Events Center in Santiago de Compostela. The event, promoted by DATAlife, brought together companies, knowledge centres and other players in the Galician innovation ecosystem to explore the practical applications of artificial intelligence

CITIC presented five demonstrator projects in the Health and Care sector, showing how data, artificial intelligence, advanced visualisation and quantum computing can be applied in healthcare.

Among them, the quantum computing and sleep medicine group presented an application of quantum recurrent neural networks to the diagnosis of sleep-disordered breathing.
