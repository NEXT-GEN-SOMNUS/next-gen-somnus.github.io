Our researchers took part this week in the second edition of the International Conference on Applied Quantum Methods in Computational Science and Engineering (AQMCSE 2026), held in the Netherlands.
The event brought together international specialists to present and discuss new quantum computing methods and their applications, from machine learning to computational finance.

In this regard, our collegue Fernando Mondragón Sampedro, presented the poster "Performance evaluation of Quantum Support Vector Machines under Realistic Noise and Mitigation". The work analyses how quantum support vector machines behave under realistic noise conditions, as well as the effect of the techniques used to mitigate it.
